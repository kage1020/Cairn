- *(core)* When one `@requires` floor named a version the edition's table cannot place, the
  `E_INTENDED_TARGET_CAP` and `W_INTENDED_TARGET_CAP` findings said no target satisfies the floors,
  which sent the author to the intent or to another floor. That floor is its own
  `E_REQUIRES_UNORDERABLE`, repaired on its own line, so it now takes no version off the list the
  note offers: the note names the targets the other floors allow, such as
  `valid bedrock targets: 1.21.60`.
